Privacy policy

Meetings NotebookLM Actions

Purpose and data access

This personal automation accesses the owner's configured Google Drive meeting folders. It reads folder and file metadata, topic configuration, prompts, and audio files. It adds audio to the chosen NotebookLM notebook and uses notebook sources to generate presentations. It writes PDF, PowerPoint, and processing-state files to the corresponding Drive output folder.

Processing and service providers

Processing runs on GitHub Actions. Audio is transferred from Google Drive to NotebookLM, and generated presentations are returned to Google Drive. Google and GitHub process data according to their applicable service terms and privacy policies. Workflow logs may contain operational details such as topic or file names and identifiers. Credentials are stored as secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are not intentionally printed in workflow logs.

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Google user data is used to perform the owner-requested meeting and presentation workflow.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Original audio remains in Drive. NotebookLM sources, generated presentations, and processing-state files remain in the owner's accounts until the owner removes them. Temporary processing files are cleaned up by the workflow. OAuth credentials remain in GitHub Secrets until the owner removes or replaces them.

The owner can revoke Google Drive authorization in the Google Account third-party access settings, disable the workflow, delete repository secrets, and delete retained content directly in Drive or NotebookLM. Revoking access does not itself delete previously saved files or notebook sources.

This information website

This static website contains no sign-in form, analytics script, advertising, or meeting content. It does not collect meeting audio or OAuth tokens. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process standard connection metadata under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and changes

The application owner is Ashooriha on GitHub. General questions can be raised in this public information repository; do not include credentials, meeting content, or other private information. This policy may be updated when the workflow changes.
